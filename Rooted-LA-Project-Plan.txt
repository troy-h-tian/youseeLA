Updated team-alignment draft
Repository supplied: https://github.com/CadenMinami/Gentrification-
Time remaining at this revision: approximately five hours
Team: two people

INTENT
Help people discover and support Inglewood businesses through an explainable
assessment that combines neighborhood numerical data with imagery that
directly influences model output.

CONFIRMED DECISIONS
- Businesses and housing tenants are equal focuses, although different aspects of the data will
contribute more or less to each type of prediction. housing- or neighbourhood-level predictions
will be targeted to nonprofits and grassroots orgs in the eventual UI, while businesses
will be targeted to customers and feature a rating system in a different UI mode.
- Inglewood is the preferred pilot area for now.
- Imagery must affect the combined support-priority assessment, not merely
  appear beside it.
- Target agreed: which areas may warrant support, not future business closure.
- Team has no local photos and proposed Google Street View as the source.
  That source is not yet cleared for analytical use.
- Languages and frameworks are flexible. No sponsor-required technology.
- Housing data and imagery must be sourced from scratch.
- All project work belongs in this workspace. Planning files are excluded by .gitignore.

IMPORTANT CHANGE FROM THE PREVIOUS PLAN
The earlier housing-only forecast plus separate visual assessment does not
meet the clarified requirement. The new model must consume visual features
alongside numerical features. The agreed target is an experimental area support-priority assessment.
A business's appearance is not evidence of its revenue or financial distress.

PRODUCT EXPERIENCE
1. Open an Inglewood business explorer with a small curated set of businesses.
2. Select a business or commercial area and view the combined assessment.
3. Inspect why it was highlighted: numerical inputs, image-derived inputs,
   image dates, and source evidence. Clearly distinguish business observations
   from neighborhood-level context.
4. Open the business website or directions to support it.
5. Optionally access tenant resources that actually serve Inglewood.

MODEL DESIGN — PROPOSED, SUBJECT TO DATA FEASIBILITY
Use an existing vision model to extract observable features from permitted,
dated images: visible for-lease signs, boarded storefronts, construction,
and explicitly uncertain observations. Associate each feature with its
business/location, date, and evidence image. Human-check demo observations.

Join visual features to housing and commercial indicators at a clearly
specified geographic scale. A combined model consumes both input types.
Do not assign one storefront's observed condition to an entire neighborhood.

Agreed target: Current area support-priority assessment. Combine neighborhood
housing/economic pressure with image-derived observable commercial conditions.
Show component scores and transparent provisional weights rather than a
probability of closure. Without independently labeled outcomes, this is an
experimental heuristic assessment, not a validated predictive model.
Businesses are recommended within assessed areas using verified context;
area-level scores must not be presented as individual business distress.

Use a consistent photo-sampling rule across compared areas, show coverage and
capture dates, and return insufficient evidence when coverage is inadequate.
Missing photos must not count as evidence that an area needs less support.

Do not train on invented labels or claim that a manually weighted formula is
an empirically validated prediction. Do not force every image to alter every
result; test whether relevant image changes can affect the combined output.

EXPLANATIONS AND EVALUATION
Show observed visual features and the numerical factors that influence the
combined result. An LLM may verbalize supplied evidence but must not invent
reasons or measurements. Feature influence does not establish causation.

For a trained forecast, hold out appropriate outcomes and compare numerical-
only, image-only, and combined models on the same held-out data. This tests
whether imagery actually adds predictive value. Report failures honestly.
For an unvalidated assessment, report sensitivity to inputs and human checks,
not accuracy percentages or probabilities of failure.

DATA STRATEGY
- Census ACS for tract-level housing/economic context. Verify historical
  periods, margins of error, missing values, and geographic boundaries.
- Dated team-owned or appropriately licensed photos. Image access is unresolved.
  Do not assume Google Street View allows the intended analytical/model use.
- Inglewood-specific business listings or verified business websites.
  Inglewood is a separate city: the City of Los Angeles business registry and
  City of LA tenant programs are not automatically applicable.
- Yelp remains optional. API credentials and review access are unconfirmed.
- Synthetic images are not real neighborhood evidence.

PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION
Python data/model scripts plus a React interface. Prefer precomputed real
model outputs for demo reliability, with source and generation dates shown.
Use a server endpoint only if live inference is necessary. Keep credentials
out of frontend bundles and version control. No paid services provisioned yet.

choose credentials/provider, and agree on the shared output format. If a

SCOPE CUTS
No satellite ingestion pipeline, citywide photo collection, accounts, payments,
custom vision training, or legal chatbot. Tenant resources are a small secondary
section. Five to ten verified business profiles are sufficient for the demo.

DEFINITION OF DONE
A visitor can inspect an Inglewood business, understand the combined result,
trace both visual and numerical inputs, and take a useful support action.
Imagery truly enters the model calculation. Claims match available evaluation.
Missing imagery is visible rather than silently imputed as reassuring evidence.
No invented business distress, causal claims, future outcomes, or performance.
