ROOTED LA — INGLEWOOD COMMUNITY SUPPORT PROTOTYPE
Repository: https://github.com/troy-h-tian/youseeLA.git
Team: two people
Budget: five-hour build allocation; actual time remaining must be rechecked.
Product name: Rooted LA remains a working name; repository name is youseeLA.

INTENT
Help customers support Inglewood businesses and help nonprofits and grassroots
organizations identify areas where housing tenants may need support, using
explainable assessments that combine numerical data with visual evidence.
Businesses and housing tenants are equal product priorities.

CONFIRMED DECISIONS
- Two equally important UI modes: business discovery for customers, and
  housing/neighborhood support for nonprofits and grassroots organizations.
- Different features and weights contribute to each assessment. One universal
  score must not stand in for both business and housing needs.
- The rating system ranks places by assessed vulnerability to gentrification-
  related displacement pressure, not customer satisfaction or review stars.
- Scores are relative exploratory assessments, not probabilities of displacement.
- Inglewood is the pilot area.
- Imagery must directly influence the assessments, not merely decorate them.
- The target is current area support priority, not future closure or eviction.
- The team has no local photos and suggested Google Street View. Analytical
  use is not cleared; a permitted imagery source remains an open dependency.
- Housing data and imagery must be sourced from scratch. Vision-model API
  access also needs setup. No required technology; frameworks are flexible.
- All project work stays in the workspace above. Scratch plans under .Codex/
  are ignored. This top-level plan is intentionally tracked and shared on
  GitHub at the user's request; .gitignore does not untrack an existing file.

PRODUCT EXPERIENCE — TWO MODES IN ONE APP
Use a shared geographic explorer with a prominent mode switch. Each mode has
its own assessment, explanation, actions, and complete demo journey.

A. SUPPORT LOCAL BUSINESSES — FOR CUSTOMERS
1. Select an Inglewood commercial area or browse five to ten verified listings.
2. View the area's business-support assessment and its evidence coverage.
3. Open a business card with verified context, the reason it is recommended,
   and clearly labeled ratings as defined below.
4. Inspect relevant numerical and visual evidence, including dates and sources.
5. Open the business website or directions to take a practical support action.
An area's assessment describes its context; it does not establish the financial
condition of any listed business.

B. HOUSING SUPPORT — FOR NONPROFITS AND GRASSROOTS ORGANIZATIONS
1. Select or compare a small set of Inglewood areas with adequate data.
2. View a housing-support assessment, component indicators, imagery coverage,
   and uncertainty. Show the precise tract or corridor boundary being assessed.
3. Inspect why an area merits further investigation: housing affordability
   indicators and relevant, verified visual observations.
4. Open a concise evidence summary and locally applicable tenant-service or
   outreach resources to inform follow-up. This is an investigation aid,
   not an automatic determination of aid eligibility or an allocation mandate.
Do not identify individual households as vulnerable based on appearance.
Housing mode is a full assessment workflow, not a secondary links section.

RATING SYSTEM — GENTRIFICATION-RELATED VULNERABILITY
Purpose: help users find places facing greater assessed displacement pressure
and understand why they are highlighted. Customer satisfaction stars are not
the product rating. Yelp reviews, if used, are optional business context only.

Use two clearly named assessments:
- Commercial-area vulnerability: indicators relevant to local business
  displacement pressure, presented in the customer/business mode.
- Housing-area vulnerability: indicators relevant to tenant displacement
  pressure, presented in the nonprofit/grassroots mode.

Display the factors behind each assessment and rank only areas with comparable,
adequate coverage. Proposed display: relative vulnerability tiers, explanation,
and evidence coverage shown separately. Thresholds and normalization remain
provisional and must be documented; do not invent probability percentages.
Do not call a place low-risk simply because information is missing.

A business card can say that the business is located in an area with higher
assessed pressure and explain its verified local relevance. The area rating
must be labeled as area context, not a claim that the individual business will
close or be displaced. A business-specific vulnerability rating would require
additional independently supported business-level indicators; none are yet
confirmed. The MVP ranks areas and helps people discover businesses within them.

The assessment is an experimental proxy for vulnerability, not proof that
gentrification is occurring or caused a particular condition. Existing poverty,
older buildings, construction, or visual vacancy alone do not establish
vulnerability to gentrification. Combine affordability/change indicators with
relevant contextual evidence and explain alternative interpretations.

MODEL DESIGN — SHARED EVIDENCE, TWO DISTINCT ASSESSMENTS
1. Vision extraction: an existing vision model extracts observable features
   from permitted dated images, with location, evidence reference, and explicit
   uncertainty. Human-check every observation used in the demonstration.
2. Numerical preparation: join housing/economic indicators and available
   commercial records to a stated geographic unit. Retain dates, missingness,
   source definitions, and uncertainty where available.
3. Two assessment configurations consume relevant numerical and visual features:
   one for commercial-area support and one for housing-area support.
4. Export both results with their own component contributions, coverage, and
   limitations. Show the same evidence differently when its relevance differs.

Commercial candidates: housing/economic context, verified commercial records,
visible storefront for-lease signage, and corroborated commercial vacancy.
A closed shutter alone is not evidence that a business has closed.

Housing candidates: rent burden, renter share, income and rent trends, plus
relevant visual observations of residential construction or redevelopment.
Construction alone does not indicate harm. Use it as a change signal whose
meaning depends on context; do not automatically increase need. Residential
imagery must actually cover the housing area being assessed. If the available
images show only storefronts, that does not satisfy the housing imagery goal.

Finalize feature definitions, directions, normalization, provisional weights,
and minimum coverage before inspecting which places rank highest. Avoid
choosing weights merely to produce a desired story. A high score means priority
under these stated assumptions, not proof of gentrification or business distress.

Without independently labeled outcomes, these are experimental heuristic
assessments rather than validated predictive models. Explain the role of the
vision model and scoring rules honestly. Do not claim that two assessment
configurations are two independently trained forecasting models.

Use consistent image sampling across compared areas; retain capture dates and
avoid counting near-duplicate photos as independent evidence. Do not assign a
single storefront observation to a whole neighborhood. Match dates as closely
as feasible and disclose temporal gaps. Missing or stale images must not count
as evidence of low need; return insufficient evidence where required.

EXPLANATIONS AND EVALUATION
For each mode, show numerical and visual contributions, source dates, spatial
coverage, provisional weights, and limitations. An LLM may phrase explanations
from supplied evidence but must not invent measurements or causes.

Check that relevant image-feature changes can affect each combined assessment.
Compare numerical-only, image-only, and combined outputs as a sensitivity
exercise, not proof of accuracy. Check ranking stability under plausible weight
changes and flag unstable results. Human-review extracted image features.
Report missing-data behavior and disagreement rather than fabricated accuracy,
confidence percentages, probabilities of failure, or causal conclusions.

A future validated prediction project would require labeled outcomes, suitable
held-out evaluation, and evidence that imagery improves performance. That is
outside this prototype's agreed target.

DATA STRATEGY
- Census ACS for tract-level housing/economic context. Check periods, margins
  of error, missing values, and geographic boundaries. Do not treat overlapping
  five-year estimates as independent yearly measurements.
- Permitted, dated commercial AND residential images. Google Street View use
  is unresolved; do not assume screenshots or manually copied observations
  bypass restrictions. Investigate Mapillary access, license obligations,
  Inglewood coverage, capture dates, and suitability before depending on it.
- Inglewood business listings and verified websites for business context.
  Inglewood is a separate city; City of LA registries and programs cannot be
  assumed to cover it. Independently verify resource service areas.
- Yelp customer ratings/reviews are optional subject to authorized access.
- Synthetic data and photos are labeled fixtures, not real assessment evidence.

PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION
Python data/model scripts plus one React interface with two UI modes. Share
geography, evidence storage, and reusable UI components; keep assessment
configuration and explanations separate. Prefer precomputed real outputs for
reliable demos, with generation dates. Add a server endpoint only if live
inference is needed; credentials never enter frontend bundles or commits.
No paid services have been provisioned.

Suggested output fields: area identifier and boundary reference, assessment
mode, component scores/contributions, source and image references, capture and
data periods, missingness, coverage status, limitations, and relevant actions.
Business records separately store recommendation rationale and the associated
area vulnerability rating, clearly identified as area context. Agree on this contract before splitting implementation.

SCOPE CUTS
No satellite ingestion pipeline, citywide photo collection, accounts, payments,
custom vision-model training, public review submissions, or legal chatbot.
No individual business closure, eviction, income, or financial-distress claims.
Five to ten business profiles and a small number of adequately covered areas
are sufficient. Keep both modes narrow and functional rather than making
housing a secondary section.

DEFINITION OF DONE
- Customer flow: select an area, inspect its business-support assessment, open
  a verified business profile, understand its area vulnerability rating, and take a support action.
- Organization flow: inspect/compare housing areas, understand the housing
  assessment and evidence gaps, and reach relevant resources for follow-up.
- Both outputs incorporate relevant visual and numerical features; image
  influence is checked separately for each mode.
- Users can trace conclusions to sources, dates, component contributions,
  provisional assumptions, and geographic coverage.
- Missing imagery and insufficient evidence produce an explicit unrated state.
- Vulnerability ratings are not presented as customer stars or calibrated
  probabilities, and area ratings are not mislabeled as business-specific risk.
- No invented reviews, business distress, tenant vulnerability, causal claims,
  future outcomes, or performance. Assessments are labeled experimental.
- Both end-to-end journeys run reliably in the demo.
