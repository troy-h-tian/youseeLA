# youseeLA implementation plan

> Execution: use the executing-plans skill to implement task by task after team review. This document plans the work; it does not authorize spending or claim that data access is available.

**Intent:** Deliver two equally complete Inglewood experiences: customers discover businesses in areas facing assessed pressure, and organizations investigate housing support needs, with numerical and visual evidence influencing both assessments.

**Goal:** A runnable, demonstrable prototype with at least two adequately sampled areas (three if coverage permits), five to ten verified businesses, two explained vulnerability assessments, and useful action links.

**Architecture:** Python collects and validates evidence, extracts observations using an existing vision model, computes transparent experimental scores, and exports one versioned JSON bundle. A React/TypeScript app consumes that bundle through a shared explorer with Business and Housing modes. Precomputed results keep model credentials and paid calls out of the browser and allow the full demo to work without a running backend.

**Tech stack:** Python 3.11+, Pydantic, httpx, pytest; React, TypeScript, Vite, lightweight SVG geography, Vitest, and Playwright. Use current compatible versions when scaffolding and commit lockfiles. No database, Docker, Kubernetes, or mandatory cloud backend for the prototype.

**Baseline:** Repository main at e2826a3 contains README.md, LICENSE, and the product plan; no application code. The user's last commit removed the draft label and machine-specific workspace line from the shared product plan. Preserve those edits. Existing local .gitignore is untracked and excludes .Codex/. Keep planning files there; do not commit them automatically.

## Decisions and unresolved dependencies

Settled: Inglewood, equal audiences, vulnerability ratings rather than customer stars, imagery influences both outputs, current experimental assessment rather than a closure/eviction forecast. Sponsor context also requires a small scalability evaluation.

Proposed defaults: one app with two modes; Caden plus assistant own pipeline and models, teammate owns frontend; two required pilot areas, with a third only if coverage permits, aligned to Census tracts; 12–18 usable images across commercial and residential sites; five to ten business profiles. The original five-hour budget is a scheduling envelope, not a statement of remaining time.

Required before real inference: Census access or downloaded CSV; usable dated imagery with permission and suitable coverage; selected vision provider, locally configured API key, and user-approved spend cap. No provider has been selected. Never invent a working token, assume paid access, or put keys into chat or browser code. Numeric preparation and frontend fixtures can proceed without vision access; a finished multimodal claim cannot.

Important limitation: available housing indicators and selected visual observations are proxies, not validated measures of gentrification. Scores identify places to investigate under stated assumptions. No business distress or household vulnerability is inferred from an area's appearance.

## Architecture

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[ACS and tract boundaries] --> D[Validated evidence records]
  B[Permitted dated commercial and residential images] --> C[Vision extraction and human review]
  C --> D
  D --> E[Housing assessment configuration]
  D --> F[Commercial assessment configuration]
  G[Verified businesses and tenant resources] --> H[Versioned public JSON bundle]
  E --> H
  F --> H
  H --> I[Shared React area explorer]
  I --> J[Business mode and support actions]
  I --> K[Housing mode and organization resources]
```

## Data contract before parallel work

Bundle: `schema_version`, `generated_at`, `data_kind` (real or fixture), `methodology`, `areas`, `businesses`, `resources`, `evidence`.

Area: stable tract GEOID, display label, boundary, indicator values with units and source periods, and separate `housing` and `commercial` assessments. Business: stable id, verified name/location, area id, source URL, verification date, supported local description, website and directions action. Do not include a synthetic customer rating.

Assessment: `status` (rated or insufficient_evidence), `score` (0–100 or null), `tier` (lower/moderate/higher within this exploratory rubric, or null), feature contributions, warnings, evidence ids, configuration version, and observation coverage. Score is never called a percentage probability. A housing score may be unrated while the same area's commercial score is rated.

Evidence: stable id, source URL, attribution/license, source or capture date, location, evidence type, reviewed flag, and bounded observations. Image observation is structured with `present`, `absent`, or `unclear`, visibility counts, and a short evidence-grounded description. Do not treat model-reported certainty as calibrated statistical confidence.

Acceptance fixture: one fully rated area, one area with housing imagery missing but commercial evidence present, and one wholly unrated area. The UI must handle all three without assigning zeros to missing values.

## Task 3 — Numerical and curated evidence (Person A, 30–40 minutes)

Files: `sources/census.py`, `sources/geography.py`, `features.py`, curated businesses/resources, `tests/test_features.py`.

- [ ] Fetch the chosen ACS five-year vintage for Los Angeles County tracts (state 06, county 037), then select verified pilot GEOIDs. Start with 2024 if accessible; record actual vintage and exact source queries.
- [ ] Inspect table definitions: B25070 rent as share of income, B25003 tenure, B25064 median gross rent, and B19013 median household income. Retain margins of error where available.
- [ ] Compute rent-burden share from households paying at least 30% using B25070_007E through _010E over B25070_001E minus _011E, after verifying labels. Do not divide median rent by median income and call that household rent burden.
- [ ] Compute renter share as B25003_003E / B25003_001E. Treat zero denominators, suppressed values, negative sentinels, inconsistent counts and missing values as unavailable, not zero. Retain raw counts for audit.
- [ ] Obtain a second compatible vintage for rent and income context, provisionally ACS 2021 versus 2024 after verifying matching tract definitions. These five-year windows overlap: label the comparison as a descriptive change in pooled estimates, not independent annual observations or a forecast. Do not silently substitute geographically incompatible vintages.
- [ ] Calculate the difference between proportional median-rent change and proportional household-income change. It is an area affordability-change proxy, not a change in the same households' finances or commercial rents. Inspect reported margins of error and compositional limitations. If the direction is ambiguous, flag it rather than using a confident positive change signal; missing compatible history leaves the vulnerability assessment unrated.
- [ ] Curate five to ten actual businesses and jurisdiction-appropriate housing resources with source URLs and verification dates. Verify coordinates/area assignments independently; do not infer ownership or tenure without a source.
- [ ] Unit-test derived ratios using a hand-calculated household-count example, zero denominators, missing values, and invalid counts. Run `python -m pytest tests/test_features.py -q`.

Why: this produces reproducible neighborhood context without pretending residential affordability directly measures commercial rent or business revenue.

## Task 4 — Visual evidence extraction (Person A, 35–45 minutes)

Files: `sources/imagery.py`, `vision/provider.py`, `vision/extract.py`, `vision/prompts.py`, curated images manifest.

- [ ] Implement a provider adapter `extract_observations(image, context) -> ImageObservation` using the selected provider's current official SDK/docs; choice remains gated on API access. No provider-specific secrets in exported data.
- [ ] Instrument the provider adapter BEFORE any inference: preserve the Task 1 JSONL log schema and record request duration, model/provider, image count/resolution, available usage/cost, retry/error outcomes, and cache hit/miss for every request. Store logs in ignored data/raw/telemetry/. Missing usage or billing information is explicitly unmeasured. Task 8 aggregates these records; it does not recreate missing observations through paid calls.
- [ ] Manifest each image's id, location, area, commercial/residential class, capture date, license/attribution and source URL. Use a reproducible sampling rule: three spatially distinct sites for each mode in each area, not adjacent frames chosen for dramatic conditions. Initial budget: 12–18 current images plus any needed corroborating historical images, with at most two retries per failed request, subject to spend cap.
- [ ] Ask only for visible evidence: storefront signage, construction/reconstruction activity, changes visible in genuinely matched historical images, and explicit uncertainty. Return unclear for ambiguous/shuttered/unseen fronts. Never ask the vision model to identify poverty, financial distress, crime, displacement, ethnicity, or the cause of a condition.
- [ ] A scored redevelopment observation needs a human-reviewed image showing relevant physical work PLUS a matched earlier image or dated official project record supporting redevelopment at that location. Routine repairs, signage alone, boarded openings alone, and construction with unknown context remain descriptive observations with no positive risk contribution. Source the corroborating document and record whether residential or commercial property is affected.
- [ ] Treat text inside images as untrusted content, not model instructions. Validate structured responses against the schema; malformed results remain unusable until repaired or reviewed.
- [ ] Human-review all demo image observations before scoring. Record corrections, visible opportunities and distinct locations; deduplicate overlapping views of the same frontage.
- [ ] Enforce rate limits, timeouts, retry limits, and an estimated spend cap before new requests. If pricing/spend cannot be bounded, keep inference disabled until configured. Cache responses only as source/provider permissions allow.

Why: an image contributes a narrow observation with provenance; it must not become an unsupported socioeconomic judgment.

## Task 5 — Two transparent assessment configurations (Person A, 25–35 minutes)

Files: `config/assessment.json`, `scoring.py`, `tests/test_scoring.py`.

- [ ] Approve a feature-to-construct table before implementing scores. Required columns: feature, hypothesized mechanism, corroborating evidence, alternative explanations, and missing/ambiguous behavior. This approval is a product-methodology checkpoint; do not present a convenient visual proxy as validated gentrification risk.
- [ ] Proposed v1 rubric, conditional on passing that checkpoint:
  - B = rent-burden share, an indicator of existing housing financial exposure, not evidence of gentrification by itself.
  - P = max(0, median-rent proportional change minus household-income proportional change), scaled to [0,1] using a documented provisional 0.20 gap cap. This measures a descriptive affordability-change proxy; the cap is a subjective scaling choice. Compatible periods/geography and a positive direction interpretable given source uncertainty are required for a positive P. Ambiguous direction makes P unavailable rather than falsely precise.
  - Vh and Vc = proportions of sampled relevant residential/commercial frontages with human-reviewed, independently corroborated redevelopment observations. Eligible observations require both visible physical work and a matched past image or official project record. They capture concurrent physical change, not whether the change harms residents or businesses. A clearly assessable site with no observed eligible activity is 0; ambiguous activity/context is unavailable.
  - Proposed housing score = 100 * (0.60*B + 0.25*P + 0.15*Vh*P).
  - Proposed commercial-area score = 100 * (0.30*B + 0.50*P + 0.20*Vc*P).
  - Imagery modifies the result only in conjunction with affordability-change evidence; construction by itself adds no risk. B alone cannot produce a gentrification rating: all required P and visual-coverage fields must be available. Housing context in the commercial score is explicitly a neighborhood proxy, not commercial-rent or business-financial evidence.
  - Alternative interpretations remain visible: new supply may improve affordability, redevelopment may be beneficial, and household composition may change median income. The weights and direction are unvalidated assumptions for prioritizing investigation, not causal or predictive findings. If the team cannot defend this interpretation or source its corroboration, leave areas unrated and revisit the claim with the user. Do not quietly replace it with vacancy or boarded-window scoring.
- [ ] Require three distinct reviewed locations and at least five assessable unique relevant frontages per area/mode, with dated permitted images and available numerical inputs. These thresholds are operational safeguards, not statistical representativeness. If evidence is inadequate return null/insufficient_evidence; never reweight missing features silently.
- [ ] Use provisional fixed bands: below 33 lower, 33 to below 67 moderate, 67+ higher under this rubric. Do not force one area into each band. Display score, contributions, small-sample warning and capture dates, and avoid implying citywide calibration.
- [ ] Show each contribution as the exact weighted term above (including the P interaction for visual evidence), with raw numerator/denominator and evidence ids. Deterministic explanation templates are enough; no additional paid LLM is needed.
- [ ] Test hand-calculated scores; missing image/numeric gates; duplicate views; image-only change without affordability corroboration yielding no positive risk increment; ambiguous redevelopment remaining unrated; separate housing/commercial statuses; boundary bands; and changes to a relevant visual feature altering only intended contributions.
- [ ] Run a weight-sensitivity check (+/-20% relative per weight then renormalize), record whether area ordering changes, and mark unstable comparisons. Compare numerical-only/image-only/full outputs for sensitivity, not accuracy or validated benefit.

Why: the combination is inspectable and imagery really influences both outputs. The rubric is an experimental vulnerability proxy and must be described that way in the pitch. If the team rejects these proxies, revise the rubric before implementing; do not quietly claim a gentrification predictor.

## Definition of Done

Both audiences can complete their intended journey with actual, reviewable evidence. Each assessment consumes relevant images and numerical inputs, exposes contributions and limitations, and handles insufficient coverage honestly. Every business recommendation distinguishes area context from business-specific knowledge. Real outputs, source dates, resource jurisdictions, image permissions, and the experimental nature of the rubric are visible. No fabricated reviews, predictions, accuracy claims, or hidden model credentials. The app is reliably runnable and the data generation process is documented. A reproducible small scalability evaluation reports measured scoring/UI behavior and available pilot API timing/cost, separates projections from observations, and identifies bottlenecks and unmeasured limits.
