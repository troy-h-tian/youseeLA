# Frontend handoff

Consume `data/exports/demo.json` for the real baseline. It currently contains 30 full Census tracts intersecting Inglewood, five verified business locations, and three local resources. All real assessments are intentionally unrated. Use `data/fixtures/bundle.json` to develop rated, missing, and partially covered states; prominently label fixture mode as synthetic.

The authoritative contract is `contracts/bundle.schema.json`, generated by `python -m youseela.cli schema`. Bundle fields include `schema_version`, `generated_at`, `assessed_as_of`, `data_kind`, `methodology`, `areas`, `businesses`, `resources`, and `evidence`.

Join business `area_id` to area `id`. GeoJSON coordinates are longitude, latitude; individual business fields are named `longitude` and `latitude`. Areas have separate `housing` and `commercial` assessments. Business mode uses the commercial **area context**, never a claim that the business is financially distressed. Nonprofit mode uses housing assessment. Neither score is a customer review rating or a probability.

For each assessment:

- `status == "insufficient_evidence"`: render “Insufficient evidence,” reasons, and coverage. Score and tier are null. Never coerce null to zero or rank it as low risk.
- `status == "rated"`: show the provisional 0–100 score, tier, component contributions, and warnings. Contribution points sum to score.
- Resolve `evidence_ids` through the evidence array to show source links, capture dates, attribution, and reviewed observations.
- Show full-tract scope and boundary vintage. The polygons are not clipped city-only estimates.

Business cards can use verified website and directions links even when unrated. Resources are Inglewood-specific pointers, not promises of eligibility or legal advice. Public URLs must not contain secrets. No frontend API keys are needed for precomputed bundles.

A teammate can copy the selected bundle into their app's public directory at build time. Check both modes and all three fixture states, a business-to-area join, evidence drill-down, and one external support action. Frontend load/render benchmarks remain the frontend owner's task; backend timing does not measure browser performance.

Historical fallback and recent-coverage results are documented in [evidence status](evidence-status.md). The historical context bundle uses the same Bundle schema. The separate image review packet is unreviewed and must not be used to populate rated assessments. Keep historical dates prominent and avoid suggesting current businesses existed at the same sites in 2019.
